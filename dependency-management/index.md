Dependencies let a project reuse libraries and tools. Learn to make installations predictable, evaluate additions, and update packages without losing track of compatibility.

## What You Will Learn

- Distinguish direct dependencies, transitive dependencies, and lockfiles.
- Isolate project environments.
- Review updates with behavior and security in mind.

## Learning Path

1. [Manifests and Lockfiles](manifests-and-lockfiles.html)
2. [Isolated Development Environments](isolated-environments.html)
3. [Evaluating and Updating Dependencies](updating-and-evaluating-dependencies.html)

Work through the lessons in order, then complete the final practice task. Each lesson includes an example and a way to check your understanding.
