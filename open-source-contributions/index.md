Contributing to open source combines technical work with the project’s collaboration practices. Learn to understand the rules, propose a useful change, and follow through respectfully.

## What You Will Learn

- Read project contribution and licensing guidance.
- Prepare a minimal, reproducible issue or patch.
- Work with maintainer feedback and ongoing releases.

## Learning Path

1. [Finding a Useful Contribution](finding-a-contribution.html)
2. [Issues, Forks, and Patches](issues-forks-and-patches.html)
3. [Working with Maintainers](working-with-maintainers.html)

Work through the lessons in order, then complete the final practice task. Each lesson includes an example and a way to check your understanding.
