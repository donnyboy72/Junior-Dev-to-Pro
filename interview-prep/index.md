Interview preparation is practice in explaining your reasoning and experience. Build evidence of practical skills, rehearse problem solving, and learn to assess the team you may join.

## What You Will Learn

- Explain a project through decisions and evidence.
- Practice coding and debugging aloud.
- Prepare concrete behavioral examples and useful questions.

## Learning Path

1. [Explaining Your Projects](explaining-your-projects.html)
2. [Coding and Debugging Interviews](coding-and-debugging-interviews.html)
3. [Behavioral Interviews and Team Fit](behavioral-interviews-and-team-fit.html)

Work through the lessons in order, then complete the final practice task. Each lesson includes an example and a way to check your understanding.
