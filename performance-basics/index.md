Performance work begins with a user-visible problem and a measurement. Learn to locate bottlenecks, evaluate changes, and avoid optimizations that make the system harder to maintain.

## What You Will Learn

- Distinguish latency, throughput, and resource use.
- Measure representative workloads.
- Choose and verify a targeted improvement.

## Learning Path

1. [Measuring Performance](measuring-performance.html)
2. [Finding Bottlenecks](finding-bottlenecks.html)
3. [Optimizing and Verifying](optimizing-and-verifying.html)

Work through the lessons in order, then complete the final practice task. Each lesson includes an example and a way to check your understanding.
