Many early-career problems come from habits around uncertainty, scope, and verification. Learn to recognize them and replace them with practical routines.

## What You Will Learn

- Ask for help with useful evidence.
- Avoid unnecessary complexity and hidden assumptions.
- Verify behavior and learn from mistakes.

## Learning Path

1. [Getting Stuck and Asking for Help](getting-stuck-and-asking-for-help.html)
2. [Scope, Assumptions, and Overengineering](scope-and-assumptions.html)
3. [Verification and Accountability](verification-and-accountability.html)

Work through the lessons in order, then complete the final practice task. Each lesson includes an example and a way to check your understanding.
