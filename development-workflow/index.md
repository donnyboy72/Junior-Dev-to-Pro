Professional development connects a request to a verified result. Practice reducing uncertainty, delivering a small change, and handing it off with enough context for others.

## What You Will Learn

- Turn requests into observable acceptance criteria.
- Implement and verify a small vertical slice.
- Communicate blockers and release outcomes.

## Learning Path

1. [Understanding the Task](understanding-the-task.html)
2. [Implementing and Verifying a Change](implementing-and-verifying.html)
3. [Communication and Delivery](communication-and-delivery.html)

Work through the lessons in order, then complete the final practice task. Each lesson includes an example and a way to check your understanding.
