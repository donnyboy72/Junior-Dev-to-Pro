Logs explain events, metrics describe trends, and traces connect work across components. Learn to use these signals to detect problems and investigate them without exposing private data.

## What You Will Learn

- Write structured diagnostic events.
- Choose useful service metrics and alerts.
- Follow a failure from symptom to evidence.

## Learning Path

1. [Writing Useful Logs](writing-useful-logs.html)
2. [Metrics and Alerts](metrics-and-alerts.html)
3. [Investigating with Logs, Metrics, and Traces](investigating-with-observability.html)

Work through the lessons in order, then complete the final practice task. Each lesson includes an example and a way to check your understanding.
